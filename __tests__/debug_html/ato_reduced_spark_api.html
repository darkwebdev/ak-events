Main article: Headhunting/Banners/2026 § Limited Headhunting
  • The fourth Limited Headhunting - Carnival banner, Hanabi Exiled, is featured.
    • Hanabi Exiled also removes Ch'en the Holungday from the secondary rate-up Operators on Limited Headhunting - Carnival banners.
    • A single Ballad of Vagabonds Headhunting Permit can be claimed while Hanabi Exiled is up.
    • Hoshiguma the Breacher can be claimed for free after the player had pulled 300 times in Hanabi Exiled.
    • Every day, the player can perform one headhunting pull for free in Hanabi Exiled. These free pulls do not transfer over to the next day, so they must be used before the daily reset.
  • The amount of Headhunting Data Contracts needed to buy Ch'en the Holungday in the Headhunting Data Contract Store is reduced to 200.
